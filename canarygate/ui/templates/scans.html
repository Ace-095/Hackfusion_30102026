What a run contains

  • Endpoint, actor and role, target resource and verified owner.
  • Expected access plus its provenance, so a disagreement is auditable.
  • Separate ownership and disclosure evidence, each with a redacted summary and SHA-256 of that summary.
  • A ten-key decision checklist. A CONFIRMED verdict requires the ownership and disclosure keys; not every key has to be true.
  • Constraint counters: out-of-scope, destructive, external and redirect counts.
