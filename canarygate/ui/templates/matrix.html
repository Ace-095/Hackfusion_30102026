Reading a cell

  • expected_access is a claim with provenance. When it is unknown, no CONFIRMED verdict is possible.
  • SAFE means the behaviour matched the expected access for that actor; it is only claimed when the oracle actually ran.
  • INCONCLUSIVE and SKIPPED are honest gaps. They are shown, never folded into SAFE.
  • BFLA cells are P1 and are not produced by this build.
