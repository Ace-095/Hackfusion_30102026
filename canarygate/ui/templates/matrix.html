{{ icon("info") }} Reading a cell

  • expected_access is a claim with provenance. When it is unknown, no CONFIRMED verdict is possible.
  • SAFE means the behaviour matched the expected access for that actor, and only when the oracle actually ran.
  • INCONCLUSIVE and SKIPPED are honest gaps; they appear in the grid rather than being hidden.
  • BFLA cells are P1 and are not produced by this build; the role column records only what a role probe observed.
  • The backend matrix distinguishes five states (red, orange, green, grey, hatched). Only a tested cell carries a verdict, and source_ref names the run that produced it.
