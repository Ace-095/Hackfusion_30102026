{{ icon("info") }} What the reports contain

  • Report view — the backend's nine-section projection rendered in-app and printable: executive summary, scope and rules, inventory, findings, secured endpoints, skipped/inconclusive, matrix, interventions and constraints.
  • Backend HTML — the same data rendered by the backend itself.
  • JSON — the stored run, the section projection, or (for fixture runs) a bundle including the minimized observation set.
  • Every report states its mode. Fixture and transcript reports are not live-target evidence; TargetLab reports are loopback-only controlled validation.
