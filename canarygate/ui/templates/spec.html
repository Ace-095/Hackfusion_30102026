{{ icon("lock") }} Why uploads are separate from scans

  • The fixture scanner is bound to the bundled synthetic fixture and cannot be pointed at an uploaded specification.
  • Uploaded documents are not persisted, so they cannot become stored evidence.
  • The response echoes parameter details from the document; that is not a production secret-redaction system.
  • Connecting an uploaded specification to a live scan requires the guarded runner, a written scope and organizer authorization — not implemented.
