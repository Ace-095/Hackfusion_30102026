{{ icon("shield") }} How a finding is allowed to exist

  • CONFIRMED requires proof that the resource belongs to the other account and proof that the acting account received that account's private data. A 200 response alone is never enough.
  • SAFE is only recorded when the oracle ran and matched the expectation, including a verified denial.
  • INCONCLUSIVE / SKIPPED stay visible here so coverage gaps cannot be mistaken for security.
  • Every finding carries a redacted request/response summary with its own SHA-256; the matrix shows which combinations were assessed.
